letsfederate.org — content architecture blueprint

How the advocacy site is organized: three story arcs × three audiences × guided journeys, with every piece of content traceable to a demonstrable fedmgr capability (or an honestly labelled roadmap item). This is the map; the AI-infrastructure paper is the plumbing and the deep re-assessment is the raw material. Dated 2026-07-05.

1 · The spine: three arcs

Every page, Q&A entry, and demo belongs to exactly one arc, so a visitor always knows which question they're answering. The arcs came straight out of the audit's websiteAngle harvest — the fixes to fedmgr's real gaps are the demonstrations.

ArcThe promiseThe proof (from the audit)
Deploy OIDF with AI Stand up verifiable trust for MCP and watch a chain resolve, admit, and revoke. The five-minute enroll→issue→admit→revoke loop; the hop-by-hop chain walk showing which key verifies which statement.
Bring your own trust You choose the anchors; no vendor is the chokepoint. A badge anyone can mint is not trust. The forged-jku trust-mark being rejected because its issuer has no chain to the anchor; the multi-anchor accepted-list.
Supply-chain integrity A trust mark proves this exact artifact passed your gate, verifiable offline. Digest-bound in-toto binding of SBOM + scan + mark; fedmgr signing its own release; the honest CBOM.

3 · Guided journeys

Journey A — "Curious → convinced" (local dev)

Home hero ("verify, don't assume") → Ask the docs: "can I try this locally?" → deploy-in-five-minutes answer → the enroll→issue→admit→revoke loop (CI-run demo) [roadmap: examples/02-trusted-mcp] → GitHub / fedmgr init

Journey B — "Assess → plan" (enterprise)

Scorecard (10 questions) → tier + per-section bars + guidance → weakest section deep-dive (e.g. supply-chain binding, revocation) → "who runs what" + "authenticate at scale" → (opt-in) guided AI assessment tier [roadmap: Dify+Langfuse] → contact / commercial letsfederate.com

Journey C — "Verify the claims" (operator)

Deep re-assessment (honest gaps, ranked) → oidf-spec conformance answers (trust_mark_type, resolve endpoint) → the trust-chain walkthrough → paste-a-trust-mark widget [roadmap: needs trust_mark_type on read] → GitHub issues / spec discussion

4 · The scorecard as the capture instrument

The scorecard is how we learn whether the thesis lands. It maps 1:1 onto the arcs, so a low score in a section routes the visitor to that arc's content, and the anonymous aggregate tells us which sections the market is weakest in (and therefore where our advocacy should concentrate).

Scorecard sectionFeeds arcWhat a low score triggers
Identity & accessDeploy OIDF with AIRoute to auth-at-scale; "start with a managed OIDC IdP".
Trust decisionsBring your own trustRoute to chain walkthrough + anchor model.
Supply-chain integritySupply-chain integrityRoute to SBOM→sign→gate→bind; "start with syft in CI".
AI & MCP exposureDeploy OIDF with AIRoute to gateway model; CBOM for the PQC question.

Capture posture (your decision): anonymous aggregate — client-side scoring, and on explicit consent a POST of answers + tier + pct only (no identity, no free text, credentials omitted). The endpoint is a Cloudflare Worker → D1; locally it's a no-op preview so the scorecard works offline. A weekly triage reads the aggregate + the Tier-1 "misses" (questions that retrieved poorly) + Langfuse traces from the opt-in tier, and every improvement lands as a content commit that re-mints the corpus.
